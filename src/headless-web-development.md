- faqTitle: "What is headless development, and how does it benefit my business?"
  faqDescription: "Headless development separates the front-end (presentation layer) from the back-end (content management or logic layer), allowing for greater flexibility and customization. It enables businesses to deliver content across multiple platforms web, mobile, IoT devices using APIs. At Cypherox Technologies, we leverage headless architecture to create fast, scalable, and future-proof solutions tailored to your needs."

- faqTitle: "Which CMS platforms do you use for headless development?"
  faqDescription: "We specialize in headless development using modern CMS platforms like Contentful, Strapi, WordPress (as a headless CMS), Drupal, and Sanity . These platforms provide robust APIs for seamless integration with custom front-ends built using frameworks like React, Angular, or Vue.js. Our team selects the best platform based on your project requirements."

- faqTitle: "Can headless development improve the performance of my website or app?"
  faqDescription: "Headless development improves performance by decoupling the front-end and back-end, enabling faster rendering and reduced server load. With lightweight front-end frameworks like React or Next.js , your application can deliver content dynamically and efficiently. We Technologies, we optimize every layer of your headless solution for speed and scalability."

- faqTitle: "Is headless development compatible with third-party tools and APIs?"
  faqDescription: "Headless architecture is API-driven, making it highly compatible with third-party tools such as payment gateways, CRM systems, analytics platforms, and marketing automation tools. We ensure smooth integration with external services to enhance functionality and streamline workflows."

- faqTitle: "How do you ensure my headless application is secure?"
  faqDescription: "We implement industry-standard security measures, including SSL certificates, authentication protocols (OAuth, JWT), input validation, and protection against vulnerabilities like XSS and CSRF. Additionally, we follow secure coding practices and regularly audit APIs and dependencies."

- faqTitle: "Can headless development support multi-platform content delivery?"
  faqDescription: "One of the key advantages of headless development is its ability to deliver content seamlessly across multiple platforms—web, mobile apps, smartwatches, and even IoT devices. Using APIs, we ensure your content is consistent and optimized for each platform. We design headless solutions that adapt effortlessly to diverse user experiences."

- faqTitle: "Do you provide ongoing support and maintenance for headless applications?"

During the initial consultation, we’ll provide a detailed timeline based on your specific requirements."
  
- faqTitle: "What are the key differences between traditional and headless development?"
